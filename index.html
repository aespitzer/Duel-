Exchanges

    How it works

    Both players put a card face down, then reveal together. Attack against attack is a race: weapon speed penalty plus 4 for a light attack or 9 for a heavy one, plus any penalty from your stance. Lower lands; equal means both land. Speed only matters between two attacks.

    Your revealed card stays on the table as your stance and changes your next card; the card it replaces goes back to your hand. Every card is always available.

    Online play is peer to peer. Each player commits to a hashed card first and only reveals once both hashes are in, so nobody can wait to see the other's card.
